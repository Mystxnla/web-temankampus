## Brand & Style

This design system serves Indonesian university students navigating the intersection of rigorous academic demands, social life, and mental well-being. The visual personality balances the clarity of modern productivity software with the soothing, non-punitive warmth of a restorative wellness sanctuary. It counters the anxiety-inducing, high-friction interfaces typical of academic portals by creating a spacious, breathable digital campus companion.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
The style is an evolution of **Tactile Soft Minimalism** merged with contemporary **Gen-Z Warmth**:
- Generous internal padding and wide macro-whitespace to reduce immediate visual stress and cognitive clutter.
- Continuous, super-ellipse curvatures (`rounded-2xl`, `rounded-3xl`, and continuous pill forms) that feel organic, forgiving, and tactile.
- Low-contrast, diffused ambient shadowing layered on warm cream and bone-tinted surfaces.
- Human, uplifting micro-copy in conversational Bahasa Indonesia (e.g., *"Yuk mulai pelan-pelan"*, *"Istirahat dulu, kamu sudah keren hari ini"*), avoiding rigid or punitive academic phrasing.

## Colors

The palette uses grounding, restorative tones to cultivate calm focus rather than urgency.

### Primary: Sage Botanical
- **Primary Base (`#4E876A`)**: Grounded herbal sage green. Used for key interactive highlights, active tab states, and primary task completion.
- **Primary Mid (`#74A892`)**: Soft eucalyptus. Used for supportive iconography, secondary highlights, and ambient hover transitions.
- **Primary Surface (`#E8F3EE`)**: Delicate mist tint. Used for primary card backdrops, progress track backgrounds, and active pill chips.

### Secondary: Soothing Sky
- **Secondary Base (`#3B82F6`)**: Bright, friendly sky blue. Applied to cognitive focus tools, revision timers, and academic schedules.
- **Secondary Mid (`#60A5FA`)**: Soft cornflower. Used for secondary graphs, calendar blocks, and sub-actions.
- **Secondary Surface (`#EFF6FF`)**: Cloud blue tint. Utilized for study-session zones and contextual informative banners.

### Tertiary & Accents
- **Lavender Slate (`#6366F1`) & Wash (`#EEF2FF`)**: Dedicated to mental health tracking, mood journaling, sleep check-ins, and reflective introspection.
- **Calm Coral (`#F87171`) & Wash (`#FEF2F2`)**: Reserved strictly for mindful reminders, deadlines within 24 hours, and gentle nudges. Avoid saturated alarm reds.

### Neutrals & Foundation
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF9F6`)**: Warm rice cream surface preventing ocular fatigue during prolonged late-night study sessions.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure cloud white for elevated cards and modals to preserve natural contrast against the cream canvas.
- **Muted Border (`#E5E7EB`)**: Subtle, non-intrusive container bounding lines.
- **Text Headings (`#1F2937`)**: Soft charcoal slate, maintaining WCAG AAA readability without the harsh contrast of pitch black.
- **Text Body (`#4B5563`)**: Balanced neutral for fluid legibility.
- **Text Muted (`#9CA3AF`)**: Understated tone for metadata, time stamps, and placeholder cues.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the single typographic family across display, body, and interface labels. Its contemporary Indonesian heritage, open counter spaces, and rounded terminals align with the platform's friendly, conversational tone.

### Scaling & Rhythm
- **Display & Large Headlines**: Set with tight negative letter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to maintain a clean, editorial look on supportive greetings and progress summaries.
- **Mobile Adaptations**: Any heading scale above `28px` adjusts dynamically using the `-mobile` token variations to preserve visual hierarchy on compact viewports.
- **Body & Captions**: Proportional line heights (1.5x - 1.6x) ensure effortless reading during extended study logging or reflective journaling.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an expansive 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to eliminate clutter and provide restful visual breaks between academic tasks and wellness insights.

### Grid Anatomy & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (`>= 1024px`)**: 12-column fluid grid, `1200px` max-width container, `gutter` of `1.5rem` (24px), outer `margin` of `2rem` (32px).
- **Tablet (`768px - 1023px`)**: 8-column layout, `gutter` of `1.25rem` (20px), outer `margin` of `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Mobile (`< 768px`)**: 4-column flow, `gutter-mobile` of `1rem` (16px), outer `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem` (20px).

### Spacing Philosophy
Interfaces must honor whitespace as an active component of student wellness:
- **Card Padding**: Never drop below `space-lg` (24px) on desktop or `space-md` (16px) on mobile.
- **Section Rhythm**: Distinct functional domains (e.g., Daily Check-in vs. Assignment Tracker) are separated by `space-2xl` (56px) to avoid cognitive fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects deep, murky black dropshadows in favor of **Tonal Diffused Ambient Layers** that evoke natural light bouncing off matte paper.

### Depth Levels
- **Flat Ground (Canvas)**: Non-elevated surface tone (`#FAF9F6`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Modules)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(78, 135, 106, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.03)`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(229, 231, 235, 0.6)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Dropdowns, Floating Navigation, Popovers)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(78, 135, 106, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Modals, Overlays)**: Pure white with soft backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md` with `rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.8)` background tint) and `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-soft, friendly visual vocabulary using `roundedness: 3` (pill and hyper-curved standards):
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Tooltips)**: `1rem` (16px, `rounded-2xl`).
- **Cards, Panels, & Modules**: `1.5rem` to `2rem` (24px - 32px, `rounded-3xl`).
- **Interactive Triggers, Buttons, Status Chips, & Reaction Tags**: Fully rounded continuous pills (`9999px`, `rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Full pill shape (`rounded-full`), solid Sage Green (`#4E876A`), text in `#FFFFFF` (`label-lg`). Resting shadow: subtle sage tint. Hover state transitions to `#3F7057` with slight upward translation (`-1px`).
- **Secondary / Soft**: Full pill shape, surface tint (`#E8F3EE`), text in `#4E876A`. No border. Hover shifts background to `#D8EADB`.
- **Ghost**: Transparent background, text in Charcoal Slate (`#1F2937`), hover states fill with `#F3F4F6`.

### Cards
- Constructed with `rounded-3xl` (24px or 32px radius), white background (`#FFFFFF`), ambient shadow elevation 1, and subtle 1px border (`#F3F4F6`).
- Card headers pair comfortable titles (`headline-sm`) with motivational Indonesian subtext (`body-sm` in `#6B7280`).

### Chips & Pill Tags
- **Status Badges**: Continuous pill (`rounded-full`), vertical padding `4px`, horizontal padding `12px`.
  - *Academic / In Progress*: Sky surface (`#EFF6FF`) with blue label (`#2563EB`).
  - *Wellness / Rest*: Lavender surface (`#EEF2FF`) with lavender label (`#4F46E5`).
  - *Gentle Urgency*: Soft coral surface (`#FEF2F2`) with muted coral text (`#DC2626`).

### Emoji Reaction Pills
- Interactive emotional self-check components (e.g., mood check-ins: *Lagi santai*, *Butuh rehat*, *Fokus mode*).
- Built with a subtle border (`1px solid #E5E7EB`), white interior, `rounded-full` curvature, and generous touch targets (`min-height: 44px`).
- Selected state activates a Sage Green border (`#4E876A`), Sage Surface background (`#E8F3EE`), and a gentle spring scale transition (`scale: 1.04`).

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs**: `rounded-2xl` boundaries, `#FFFFFF` field surface, border in `#E5E7EB`, font `body-md`. Focus rings use a gentle 3px diffuse ring in `#E8F3EE` with `#4E876A` border.
- **Checkboxes**: Soft `rounded-lg` (8px), unselected border `#D1D5DB`. Checked state: filled Sage Green with a smooth white checkmark icon, avoiding harsh squares.

### Calm Progress Rings & Trackers
- Track bars feature ultra-thick stroke widths (8px - 12px) with full round end caps (`stroke-linecap: round`).
- Inactive track color: `#F3F4F6` or `#E8F3EE`.
- Active fill: Sage Botanical gradient to Soothing Sky. Center layout houses an encouraging milestone metric (e.g., *"3 dari 5 tugas selesai - pelan tapi pasti"*).
