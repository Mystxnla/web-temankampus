## Brand & Style

This design system blends youthful university energy with modern digital agency aesthetics. Designed for university students balancing academic pressures with personal mental wellness, the aesthetic delivers an uplifting, non-clinical environment. It avoids dull institutional utility and overly sterile wellness tropes in favor of punchy self-expression, creative confidence, and welcoming optimism.

### Visual Aesthetic
The style is a fusion of **Neo-Pop Playfulness** and **Clean Graphic Modernism**:
- Soft, dreamy lavender and lilac canvas tones create a soothing foundational calm for student focus and stress relief.
- High-contrast ink-black condensed headings project urgency, graphic presence, and contemporary streetwise credibility.
- Electric magenta and punchy pink accents inject optimism, vitality, and gamified engagement across action triggers.
- Generously rounded containers, subtle stroke outlines, and soft tinted drop shadows maintain clarity and modern polish.

## Colors

The palette balances soothing pastel surfaces with electric high-energy highlights to maintain student focus without inducing cognitive fatigue.

### Palette Roles
- **Canvas & Backgrounds**: Layered lavender tones (`#F5EEFD`, `#EDE9FE`, `#E9D5FF`) establish calm baseline zones for dashboard feeds, journaling spaces, and academic organizers.
- **Primary Accent (`#FF2E93`)**: Bold neon magenta used for high-impact call-to-actions, wellness check-in completion states, streaks, and active tab indicators.
- **Secondary Accent (`#7C3AED`)**: Rich violet supporting deep navigational links, badges, focus timers, and secondary interactive states.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#06B6D4`)**: Crisp electric cyan for student collaborative features, peer-support notifications, and balanced utility metrics.
- **Ink & Neutral (`#0F0B1E`)**: A deep, saturated violet-black for typography and crisp structural borders, eliminating muddy pure blacks while preserving strict WCAG AAA contrast against lilac canvases.
- **Surface Elevation Containers (`#FFFFFF` with `#D8B4FE` borders)**: Crisp white islands nesting directly on lilac canvas tiers to isolate task lists, mood logs, and calendar agendas.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs two contrasting personalities:
1. **Anton** delivers graphic attitude, impact, and high-energy visibility for major section headers, hero slogans, and key stats. It borrows the punchy poster-like headline style seen in cutting-edge creative studios. Anton must always be styled in uppercase or capitalized display styling.
2. **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides warm, ultra-legible clarity for body copy, micro-interactions, student schedules, wellness logs, and form elements. Its rounded, open letterforms balance Anton's vertical density with friendly human warmth.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column desktop grid transitions to a 6-column tablet grid and a 4-column mobile layout. Spacing favors generous card padding and distinct content grouping to prevent academic overwhelm.

### Layout Adaptations
- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column grid, 80px max outer gutters, 24px column gutters. Persistent lateral navigation rails or floating top-bar navigation dock.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 6-column layout with 20px gutters. Dashboard modules wrap into two-column blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Single-column scroll with 16px lateral padding. Fixed bottom navigation deck for instant thumb reach across schedule, mood tracker, study timer, and campus community.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids heavy realistic shadows. Instead, it combines crisp surface layering with tonal lavender ambient glows:

- **Base Layer (Ground)**: Textured lilac wash (`#F5EEFD`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards & Blocks)**: Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1.5px subtle border (`#E9D5FF` or `#D8B4FE`). Casts an ambient lavender drop shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.08)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Modals & Popovers)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a crisp 2px border in `#C084FC` and an offset shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(255, 46, 147, 0.14)`.
- **Interactive State (Hover & Active)**: Micro-interaction transforms elements via a -2px upward translation and a punchy solid shadow displacement (`3px 3px 0px #0F0B1E`), providing a playful tactile response.

## Shapes

The shape system employs rounded contours (`0.75rem` for inputs and cards, extending to full pills for chips, badges, and action buttons). This softens the visual field, rendering wellness trackers and productivity dashboards welcoming and approachable. Accent stickers, avatar containers, and graphic badges can feature asymmetrical corners or pill-shaped geometries to enhance the creative student tone.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Electric pink (`#FF2E93`) background, crisp white typography in uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans (Bold/Extra-Bold), full pill radius (`9999px`), 14px vertical padding and 28px horizontal padding. Hover activates a -2px Y-translation with a punchy deep-ink drop shadow (`0 4px 0 #0F0B1E`).
- **Secondary Action**: Solid deep neutral (`#0F0B1E`) background, white text, pill radius, paired with primary buttons in dual CTA groups.
- **Tertiary / Soft Button**: Subtle lilac container (`#EDE9FE`), purple text (`#7C3AED`), pill radius.

### Cards & Containers
- Built using pure white backgrounds nested over lilac surfaces.
- Outlined with subtle 1px to 1.5px borders (`#E9D5FF`).
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg` / 24px).
- Incorporates bright, high-contrast category tags and expressive illustration slots that mirror stylized vector line art.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped tags with uppercase label typography (`label-md`).
- Mood tracker badges use color-coded soft pastels (e.g., Calm Lavender, Energized Magenta, Focused Cyan) accented with dark text and small playful icons.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- High-contrast white fields with 1.5px `#D8B4FE` borders, curving at `0.75rem`.
- Active focus state swaps border to punchy pink (`#FF2E93`) with a 3px soft outer ring (`rgba(255, 46, 147, 0.2)`).
- Input labels rendered in semi-bold Plus Jakarta Sans ink text.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded squares (for checkboxes) and circles (for radios) with 2px ink borders (`#0F0B1E`).
- Checked state fills with `#FF2E93` featuring a bold white checkmark.

### Campus Wellness & Productivity Modules
- **Focus Timer / Pomodoro Widget**: Prominent Anton numerical readout against a pale violet card with a circular pink neon progress track.
- **Mood Tracker Strip**: Row of pill-shaped interactive emoji chips that lift with tactile spring transitions on selection.
- **Academic Task Item**: Clean row container with a strikethrough transition upon completion, highlighted with a micro-confetti burst or subtle lilac background wash.
